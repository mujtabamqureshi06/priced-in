# Phase 6 spread set

Discount rates that the searched royalty and streaming companies disclose for their own gold royalty and stream interests, compiled under `docs/phase6/protocol.md` section 8.3 (step 2 of 8.8). No acquisition price or deal consideration is recorded, and no spread is computed here; conversion to spreads is done by the pricing script.

- Rows: 35; producing-asset rows (the primary set): 24 from 7 companies. The minimum of 8 rates from 4 companies is met.
- SHA-256 of `spread_set.csv`: `b839d2f4f08a37c94a42ebc57de2cecf0dba71eea163f264d8e4aece0c1565d5`.

Compilation judgments (each also in the row's notes):
- Fair values set at the date of an acquisition, disposal or restructuring are excluded, because the source must be independent of deal prices.
- Suspended or ceased mines are classed as development.
- Assets other than gold royalties and streams are excluded (silver-only, base metals, coal, iron ore, diamonds, offtake contracts).
- Several assets in one class and year form one row, from the lowest to the highest rate.
- Rates found only as comparatives in the next year's statements are recorded under their own year, citing the later document. Interim-statement rates are excluded.
- Altius Minerals' rate for the 777 royalty is excluded: 777 is a copper-zinc mine with gold as a by-product, so it is not a gold royalty interest, and the statements tie the rate to an acquisition.
