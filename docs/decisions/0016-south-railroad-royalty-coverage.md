# 0016. Placing the Vox royalty coverage in the South Railroad schedule

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-16

## Context

Vox disclosed an effective 0.633% NSR covering about 35% of the 1.9 Moz South Railroad resource (June 7, 2021). The coverage is stated against the resource, not the PFS mine plan, and the covered parcel is not disclosed. The PFS lists eleven royalties by land section (Section 4.2) but does not name this one.

## Decision

1. **Default.** A 35% covered share applied uniformly to payable gold in every year of the PFS mine plan. Royalty metals as stated by Vox; if not stated, gold only, flagged.
2. **Timing bounds.** Put 35% of total payable ounces into the earliest years first (front-loaded) and, separately, the latest years first (back-loaded), never more than 100% of a year's ounces. These are bounds on the timing effect, not scenarios. A split by deposit may be shown as an illustration only if each deposit holds enough ounces.
3. **Level uncertainty in words.** The write-up says the 35% is a share of the resource, not the mine plan, and that implied odds scale roughly in proportion to the covered share. No invented band for the level.
4. **Contract terms before the inversion.** Terms in force at the deal date may be taken from later Vox documents (annual information form, MD&A, asset handbook) without breaking ADR 0007, which covers outcomes and study updates, not terms that already existed. Check for: the covered parcel or claims; any buy-down or buyback right; whether advance minimum royalties are credited against later payments. If Vox's own disclosure identifies the parcel, a parcel-based profile replaces the uniform default through a new ADR. The seller's identity is never inferred from land-section maps.
5. **No double counting.** The PFS royalty burden (1.24% NSR after planned buy-downs) stays in the project and equity cash flows. The royalty claim receives Vox's slice. Project and royalty values are never added and called the asset's total value. If the PFS's planned buy-downs include this royalty, the operator's buy-down right is modelled in the royalty claim. If the facts cannot be established, assume no buy-down and state that this overstates the royalty value and pushes implied odds lower.

## Consequences

The South Railroad inversion is not published until the buy-down and advance-minimum checks are resolved or explicitly assumed with the direction of bias stated.
