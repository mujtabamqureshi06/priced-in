# ADR 0068: Phase 5 labels after second coding, and the primary table

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-20

## Context

Second coding under the section 5.1 rules covered 101 of 174 projects. Every disagreement was settled under the written rules, with a reason recorded per project. A component coding of the 13 linked groups, made for the table with components kept separate, raised two questions about links. The stage table was run once before the four questions below were decided, so they were decided with the rates known. The write-up says so, and every alternative is reported.

## Decision

1. **Primary table.** The linked-groups check in section 2.6 is treated as triggered: 8 of 13 linked groups ended in a construction decision, against 64 of 158 projects never linked. The table with linked components kept separate becomes the primary table, and the linked table is reported beside it. The primary table does not depend on links drawn from later documents.
2. **Bardoc.** The linked-projects rule is applied as written. Excelsior Gold's funded 2015 start at Kalgoorlie North mined Zoroastrian, a deposit in the later combined Bardoc plan, so the linked group has a decision on 9 November 2015, flagged partial and low confidence. Its 2020 PFS and 2021 FS open no entry. In the primary table the Kalgoorlie North component carries the 2015 decision, and the combined Bardoc studies, which belong to no single component, open no entry; under frame rule 6 they would also be excluded, because part of the combined plan was decided before them. Both tables are also reported without Bardoc.
3. **Links re-checked.** The component coding found that the Gruyere studies may not put the Yamarna deposits in one mine plan with Gruyere. Correcting only the link that outcome coding happened to reach would check errors only where outcomes drew attention. So a coder with no outcome information re-checked all 13 links against the written rule, and the linked table is reported both as fixed with the frame and with failed links corrected. Result: 11 links pass; Gruyere-Yamarna is partial (the 2015 study puts Gruyere and Central Bore in one plan, but not Attila South or Alaric, the open pits of the 2012 Yamarna study), so the group is split in the corrected table; Mallina is unclear because the deciding document could not be reached, and stays as fixed (its earlier component opens no entry either way). The Gruyere-Yamarna group decision is dated 15 February 2017, the last approval.
4. **Witwatersrand Basin.** No decision by the cut-off, as the committed-funding rule requires: the senior debt was a credit-approved term sheet and committed funds were below the stated peak requirement at 31 March 2026. The alternative reading is reported.
5. **Label changes.** Six event labels changed between the first labels and the final labels, more than the three that section 5.1 asks to be reported. Coders agreed on the event for 96 of 101 projects; the changes come from rules fixed after the first labels and from the linked-projects rule. They are listed in `docs/phase5/labels.md`.

## Consequences

- The headline rates do not depend on links made with later documents.
- Each choice above moves no 36-month stage rate by more than 2 points; the robustness table shows each one.

## Revisit if

- The blind re-check corrects more than three links.
- Any of these choices moves a 36-month stage rate by more than 3 points; the write-up then shows that sensitivity beside the headline.
