# 0028. South Railroad presentation and the royalty-convention anchor

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-16
- Decided by: Independent Decision Maker (owner's proxy), DR-014 and DR-015
- Amends: ADR 0014 (the spread is not fixed on one control), ADR 0026 item 4

## Context

One producing-royalty control qualifies: Wharf (Metalla, 2020; Tier 2, 81% paid in shares), implying a real spread of about 15.8% on reserve-only flows, a lower bound on the spread on expected flows. Checks (coverage, payment timing, tax basis, guidance, caps) left it unchanged. The CIM survey rate is a producing-mine convention, not royalty-specific, and is a cross-check only. A royalty-specific anchor was found in audited impairment notes. At the Wharf spread the South Railroad price is above the certain-build value at every delay; at the convention anchor it implies high odds on schedule. The two sourced anchors put South Railroad on opposite sides of the feasible region.

## Decision

1. **Convention anchor:** Osisko Gold Royalties, audited 2020 statements (published before the South Railroad valuation date), Note 19: post-tax real discount rate 3.5% for the Éléonore NSR and Canadian Malartic NSR cash-generating units. Pre-tax equivalent 3.5% / (1 - 0.265) = 4.76%, using the 26.5% combined statutory rate in Note 26 (an approximation that ignores depletion shields). Both are converted to spreads over the real risk-free rate at the target's date. The 2021 rate (4.3%) is a labelled sensitivity only (look-ahead otherwise). Label: "royalty holder's value-in-use rate for producing Canadian royalties (IAS 36), one company; not a purchase price." Franco-Nevada, Sandstorm and Royal Gold notes are to be read; two or more comparable rates turn the anchor into their range.
2. **Presentation:** South Railroad is an illustration whose main finding is that the buyer's required return dominates; not a calibrated implied-odds result. The case output has: (a) zero-delay implied odds against the real spread, marking the Osisko anchor (grossed-up and raw), Wharf's spread, and the break-even spread at which the price equals the certain, on-schedule value; (b) the odds-delay frontier at the Osisko anchor with both ADR 0023 endpoints; (c) neutral wording for the Wharf result; (d) a headline sentence on the dependence on the required return.
3. **Wharf** is a reported anchor, not the calibrated value. The spread is held fixed only once at least two qualifying controls agree within the revisit tolerance.
4. **Houndé** continues: if Endeavour's technical report yields a Tier 1 schedule, Houndé enters as a cash control with its jurisdiction note (Bouéré coverage bounded at 50% and 100%, from Sandstorm's "most").
5. **Houndé, CIM:** the CIM survey is labelled "producing-mine valuation convention; not royalty-specific".

## Conditions

- Resource and reserve figures in the wording come from the 2020 PFS and the June 2021 Vox release only. No resource-upside adjustment is made to South Railroad.
- **Owner review before publication** of the wording in items 2(c) and 2(d).

## Revisit if

A second qualifying control falls within 25 points of either anchor; two or more audited royalty-company rates define a range that excludes Osisko's; or the break-even spread's position changes the headline wording.
