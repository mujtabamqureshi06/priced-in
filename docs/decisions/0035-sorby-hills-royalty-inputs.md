# 0035. Sorby Hills royalty inputs and presentation

- Status: accepted; the conversion of reference rates to spreads (k - r) is superseded by ADR 0045
- Date: 2026-11-16

## Context

On September 28, 2026 Vox agreed to buy an "effective 0.75% NSR" over Sorby Hills (silver-lead, Western Australia) for US$13.0M cash from a private seller. Completion depends on FIRB approval. The project had a final investment decision in December 2025, was fully funded and in construction, and targeted first concentrate in H2 2027. The latest public schedule is the June 2024 FEED study, Table 10; in December 2025 Boab stated its production targets "continue to apply". The FEED is pre-tax only.

The release does not say which metals the royalty covers or explain "effective". Boab's half-year report (Note 14) discloses a 1% NSR to Quintana MH Holding Company LLC from the original acquisition, metals not stated.

## Decision

1. **Metals:** NSR on all payable metals (silver and lead) is the headline. Silver only is a stated sensitivity.
2. **Rate and coverage:** 0.75% of 100% of project NSR. The likely reconciliation, 1% on the 75% interest Boab then held, is stated as an inference.
3. **NSR base:** revenue less treatment and refining charges, both set in US$ in the FEED. Deducting logistics as well is a sensitivity.
4. **Timing:** the FEED's first production row (2027) is paid at the end of year 2 from the deal date, reflecting H2 2027 first concentrate. Year 1 is a sensitivity.
5. **Convenience yield:** y = 0 for silver and lead, as for gold (ADR 0024), with plus and minus 2% as sensitivities. Lead's y implied by the LME cash to 3-month spread is a further sensitivity, labelled a short-tenor proxy (the treatment ADR 0027 allowed).
6. **Schedule:** the FEED schedule only (8.5 years). Flag that it includes 17% Inferred material. Value beyond the FEED schedule (drilling, mine-life extension) is not modelled and is named as one reading of any gap.
7. **Presentation:** as South Railroad (ADR 0028): value and implied odds across spreads, break-even marked. Label the odds "odds of reaching production on the FEED schedule"; the construction decision has been made.
8. **Reference rates.** The Osisko (now OR Royalties) figure is an absolute real rate, not a spread. Use the latest audited rate published before the deal: 5.1% post-tax real (2025 statements, Note 12). Convert it at this deal's date by subtracting the 2.90% TIPS yield, both raw and grossed up at the 26.5% statutory rate (ADR 0028). Wharf's 15.8% is already a spread and is used as is.
9. **Project and equity values** are not reported for this case; the FEED is pre-tax only.

## Rationale

Real rates rose from -0.82% (2021) to 2.90%. A convention rate of 4% to 5% therefore converts to a spread of about 1% to 2% at this date, close to the 2.3% break-even. That makes the likely reading "consistent with near-certain completion at royalty-company rates", which is what a high-odds test case should show. Mixing a rate with a spread would have given the wrong headline.

## Conditions and limits

Possible reasons for any gap are listed in neutral wording only, as in ADR 0028.

## Revisit if

The boundaries of the covered tenements, or the metals covered, are disclosed.
