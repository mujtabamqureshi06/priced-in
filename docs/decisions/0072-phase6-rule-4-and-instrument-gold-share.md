# ADR 0072: Phase 6 rule 4 points and the instrument's gold share

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-25
- Amends: ADR 0070, ADR 0071

## Context

After second coding and resolution, two deals turned on questions about stream terms that the written rules did not answer, and two kept deals raised related questions. These points were decided with the count after second coding known. They were decided on the wording and purpose of rule 4, not on the threshold, and the count reports each both ways.

## Decision

1. **Instrument gold share.** The 80% gold share in rule 2 applies to the instrument as well as the project: for a royalty, gold's share of the revenue it is paid on; for a stream, gold's share of the value delivered under it, at study prices, with the bases and band of protocol section 7.5. A single-price stream that delivers gold and silver at a fixed ratio is assessed on this basis.
2. **Debt-linked steps.** A stream whose delivery percentage steps with the operator's debt is standard if the step is fully specified in documents dated on or before the announcement, flagged.
3. **Pre-production payments in ounces.** They count as an advance minimum royalty only if documents dated on or before the announcement show they are credited against later royalties, or are a stated minimum with a specified limit. Otherwise they are fixed-ounce deliveries and not standard (protocol section 7.3).
4. **A deal not completed** because a right of first refusal was exercised on matching terms keeps its coding, flagged, and is reported with and without.
5. **Undocumented "not usable" codings** are each checked against a primary document before the count is final.

## Consequences

- The count is 22 usable deals, a case series (`docs/phase6/count.md`).
