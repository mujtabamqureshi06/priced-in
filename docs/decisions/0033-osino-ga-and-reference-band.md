# 0033. Osino corporate G&A and the fairness-opinion reference band

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-16

## Context

Osino's own interim statements at September 30, 2023 (the latest published before the December 2023 and February 2024 prices) are available from the company's archived website. They give cash of C$8.339 million, a Nebari credit facility of C$19.676 million, leases of C$0.084 million and other financial liabilities of C$2.668 million (net debt C$14.09 million), 9.43 million options at C$1.02 and 9.25 million warrants at C$1.28, and corporate G&A of C$5.449 million for nine months. Both fairness opinions in the January 29, 2024 circular are dated December 17, 2023; Eight Capital used Twin Hills discount rates of 10.5% to 13.5% on constant-dollar cash flows at US$1,850/oz.

## Decision

1. **G&A horizon:** corporate G&A of C$7.3 million a year (the nine-month figure annualised, stated as such) is deducted for the whole mine life on built paths, and until the failure date on paths that fail, discounted at the real risk-free rate. Pre-production only is a sensitivity. It applies to market values of the developer, not to bids.
2. **Reference band:** Eight Capital's 10.5% to 13.5% is shown as a reference only, labelled "Eight Capital fairness opinion, dated 2023-12-17: constant-dollar cash flows, US$1,850 gold; basis differs from the engine's, so approximate". On the July and December 15 curves it is also marked "dated after this price".
3. The vendor balance sheet used provisionally under ADR 0032 is replaced by these primary figures; balance sheets published after a price date are not used (the December 31, 2023 balance was published after February 23, 2024).

## Revisit if

Osino disclosed guidance for corporate costs in production.
