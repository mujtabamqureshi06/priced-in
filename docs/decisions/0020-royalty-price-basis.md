# 0020. Royalty price basis: itemised consideration plus itemised fees (clarifies 0014)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-16
- Decided by: Independent Decision Maker (owner's proxy), decision record DR-007
- Clarifies: ADR 0014 ("buyer's total disclosed cost")

## Decision

"Buyer's total disclosed cost" means itemised consideration plus itemised fees, applied identically to the target and every control.

South Railroad: headline price US$2.079M (US$1.98M cash plus the US$99,000 finder's fee; Vox release, June 7, 2021; Vox audited 2022 statements, Note 6). Named sensitivity: US$2.317M, labelled "capitalised cost; includes about US$238k of transaction costs that are not itemised."

## Consequences

The 11% gap moves implied odds roughly in proportion, so it is always shown. Revisit if Vox itemises the extra US$238k.
