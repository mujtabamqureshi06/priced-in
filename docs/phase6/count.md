# Phase 6 count of usable deals

Recorded 2026-11-25, before part 2 of the protocol is written and before any deal is priced. Counts only: no price amounts and no ratios of prices to anything. The deal list itself is held privately; it can be published if the owner decides (protocol section 6).

## Result

**22 usable deals.** Under ADR 0063 Phase 6 is a **case series** (15 to 24 usable deals): every deal is reported in aggregate, and no statistic is presented as confirmatory.

## How the count was reached

| Step | Deals |
|---|---|
| Gold-project royalty and stream deals found from 23 buyers, January 2010 to October 2026 | 429 |
| Coded a second time without the first coder's marks (seed 20261125: 123 drawn at random, plus every deal the first pass found usable or unclear) | 160 |
| Agreement on usability among the 160: both usable / both not / both unclear / disagree | 19 / 112 / 14 / 15 |
| Settled under the written rules after second coding: usable | 4 of the 15 |
| Removed by the checks ordered after second coding (instrument gold share, advance minimum royalty test) | 1 |
| Usable | 22 |

Agreement by rule among the 160 doubly coded deals: price 119, pre-production gold 133, study 121, standard terms 109, one asset per price 144, related party 118.

Deals still unclear after second coding are not usable (section 7.5): 14 unclear for both coders, plus 2 left unclear on documents at resolution and 2 whose terms were not fully specified in documents dated on or before the announcement.

Every "not usable" coding made without a fetched primary document (25 deals, after the coders reached a search limit) was checked against at least one primary document afterwards: all 25 confirmed.

## Readings under each interpretation and its alternative

The interpretations in protocol section 7 (ADR 0071) and the rule 4 rulings in ADR 0072 were made after the first-pass count, and those in ADR 0072 after the count after second coding was known. Each is shown both ways.

| Interpretation | Count as ruled | Count under the alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Unvalued warrants at notional value (7.1) | 22 | 22 |
| Buy-back at fair market value standard (7.2) | 22 | 21 |
| Fixed-ounce deliveries not standard (7.3); pre-production ounce payments not an advance minimum royalty without crediting or a stated limit (ADR 0072) | 22 | 23 |
| A study counts from the first public date of its after-tax results (7.4) | 22 | 22 |
| Head-grade or contained-metal gold shares decide a deal only outside 70% to 90% (7.5) | 22 | at most 24 |
| A single-price gold and silver stream is not a gold stream (ADR 0072) | 22 | 23 |
| A deal not completed because a right of first refusal was exercised is kept | 22 | 21 |

No single interpretation moves the count to 25. All the alternatives together would give at most 26.

## Limits

- Purchases by private funds and unlisted buyers are found only where the other side disclosed them.
- One second coder saw a list of 13 deal identifiers (not codings) prepared by another second coder; none was in its own input. The slip is disclosed and no deal was recoded.
- Some coded facts rest on copies of company releases on news distribution sites where the originals could not be reached; each such row says so.
